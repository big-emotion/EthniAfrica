# Scene production brief

Copy into the private subject directory as `production-brief.md`. See the [workflow](../SCENE-PRODUCTION.md).
This document is a human handoff, not a second executable schema. The engine reads `scene-plan.json`.

## Scene decisions

| Scene / editorial beat | Spoken words or paragraph range | Visual purpose                   | Scene type and asset IDs                   | Map layers / camera       | Timed reveals         | Silent context / period | Transition                   | Evidence / uncertainty |
| ---------------------- | ------------------------------- | -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ | ------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| Fill after planning    | Exact approved words            | Why this shot supports the claim | No missing asset placeholders at execution | Reviewed coordinates only | Bind to aligned words | Optional and secondary  | Cut unless there is a reason | Source IDs and status  |

Record measured local seconds in the final plan. A paragraph can span several shots and several
paragraphs can share one sustained map. Do not retime the audio to fit an arbitrary scene slot.

## Acceptance

No changed speech; no source or licence silently substituted; no hidden historical uncertainty;
no overlapping visible labels; movement follows the approved narration; optional context stays
secondary; audio remains present and synchronized; source fingerprints and sampled pixels match;
complete media decode succeeds. Record any criterion not yet satisfied without marking it passed.
