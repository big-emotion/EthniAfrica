# Licence — beds

- **Files:** `library/<name>.wav`, one bed per cultural area: `akan`, `mandingue`, `touareg`, `amazigh`, `rumba-congolaise`, `afrique-du-sud`, `nigeria`, `afrique-centrale`. `series.json` names each series' default bed; an episode may name its own (`bed` in the piece).
- **Author:** generated with ElevenLabs Music (model music_v2, instrumental) on the operator's ElevenLabs account, 21 October 2026, from prompts asking for an instrumental underscore « in the style of » the area's music, without vocals, chants or build-ups; converted to WAV 32 kHz mono and made loopable (the first 3 s crossfaded into the end).
- **What they are not:** compositions generated in a style, not recordings of musicians or traditional pieces. They are never presented as traditional music, and an episode's bed follows the area its people belong to.
- **Licence:** use under the operator's ElevenLabs subscription terms. The operator approved ElevenLabs as the source of the series sounds on 21 October 2026 (« ok pour eleven labs »), after being asked whether the subscription covers commercial use.
- **Choice:** the operator listened to each bed alone and under narration, about 17 dB under the voice, and kept all eight (« je garde tous les fonds »), preferring the Mande and Congolese rumba beds for the series with the most episodes, 21 October 2026.
