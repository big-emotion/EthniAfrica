**The hand.** An ink line (`illustration-line`, #23170d) over a watercolour wash lit from the top left, a few engraved strokes in the shade, a soft contact shadow. Realistic colours; never an emoji, a flat pictogram, a disc badge or a symbol; generic small figures, never a portrait or a named person. On the image each drawing takes the 1.9.0 second contour (r 3).

**Format.** viewBox `0 -12 160 132`; the drawing's foot is at (80, 112) and is set on its place on the map. Its height on the map is `clamp(round(1.1 × s), 100, 150)` px at 1080 × 1920, s being the shorter side of the smallest lit area on screen; its width is four thirds of its height at most. Gradient and filter ids are prefixed with the file's name, so several drawings can share a page.
