# Flags

Flags drawn inside a state's area on the series video map (1.13.0-proposition, accepted), only for a state existing on the date shown (Series, « 8. A flag inside a territory »). Drawn by Claude Design from the official descriptions, 3:2; the adoption dates are to be sourced in each script.

- `bfa.svg`: Burkina Faso, adopted on 4 August 1984: red over green, a yellow five-pointed star.
- `hv.svg`: Republic of Upper Volta, 9 December 1959 to 4 August 1984: black, white and red, for the three Voltas.
