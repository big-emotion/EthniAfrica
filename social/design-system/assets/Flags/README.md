# Flags

Flags drawn inside a state's area on the series video map (1.13.0-proposition, accepted), only for a state existing on the date shown (Series, « 8. A flag inside a territory »). Drawn by Claude Design from the official descriptions, 3:2; the adoption dates are to be sourced in each script.

- `bfa.svg`: Burkina Faso, adopted on 4 August 1984: red over green, a yellow five-pointed star.
- `hv.svg`: Republic of Upper Volta, 9 December 1959 to 4 August 1984: black, white and red, for the three Voltas.

## Added in 1.16.0-proposition (accepted on 27 October 2026)

Four flags, usable inside a state's area under the rule above (`flags` on a card) and as a flag planted on its place (`map.drawings` with `flag`, Series, « 1.16.0-proposition: symbols, not words »). Same 3:2 viewBox `0 0 300 200`. The state each stands for is the name the voice must say (`FLAG_STATES` in `social/video/series-turns.mjs`).

- `zar.svg`: Republic of Zaïre, 1971 to 1997: a green field, a yellow disc in the centre, a brown arm and hand holding a torch with a red flame. Credited on screen as « dessin d'après la description officielle »: not traced from a reference drawing: the arm's proportions are an approximation, to be checked by the operator against a sourced image before a final export.
- `cod.svg`: Democratic Republic of the Congo, since 18 February 2006: sky blue, a red diagonal edged in yellow from the bottom left to the top right, a yellow five-pointed star at the top left. The voice names it as « Congo ».
- `fra.svg`: France: blue, white and red, vertical. A colonial power's flag is never planted or unrolled over African land (1.13.0, « 8 »).
- `bra.svg`: Brazil: a green field, a yellow rhombus, a blue globe crossed by a white band; at map size the stars are five dots and the motto is left out.
