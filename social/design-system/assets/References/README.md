Reference renders for fidelity checks: every card of `handoff/cards.sample.json`, exported at 1080 × 1350 by `EthniCards` 1.2.0-proposition with the fonts in `fonts/`. The `atlas-*` images are the 1.2.0 historical atlas samples; `atlas-proto-collisions.png` is the 12 October prototype rendered unchanged, which the collision check refuses. A renderer that implements this system should reproduce them. `overflow.png` is rendered with diagnostics on and shows the refusal state. Yellow tapes mark maquette text and fictive map areas. Since 1.3.0, the `serie-*` images are the seven series models and two band-over-map samples, rendered by `EthniCards` 1.3.0-proposition; the 1.2.0 references are unchanged by it.
