The historical atlas variant of the map card (1.2.0): approximate zones for the states, societies without a king and archaeological cultures of one moment, named on the map where they can be and numbered in keys by region where they cannot; the 1914 partition in eight colour-safe categories with its territories outlined and named; the independence map with its marks; dotted present-day borders on filled maps; and the period marker of a series. Fields and rules are in `components/MapCard/README.md`, in Maps (« Historical atlas map ») and in Handoff (« Atlas map fields »).
