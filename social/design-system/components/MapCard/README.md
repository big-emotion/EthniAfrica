The map card lets one geographic question fill the card, with a title, a legend, a one-line caption and a border layer that can be off, quiet or strong.

**Provide:** `type: "map"`, `kicker`, `title` (words may take an area's colour: `{area-a:langue A}`), and `map: {dataRef, view, borders: "none" | "quiet" | "strong", areas: [{label, legend, tone, kind: "certain" | "uncertain", paths}], labels, places, caption: {what, when, source}}`. No `body`. For a people across several states, keep `borders: "quiet"` and use `highlight`, then `callout` (continent) or `countryLabels: "highlight"` with `inset` (region). To show how many live in each state, use `choropleth` (graded classes) instead of `highlight`; see the Maps section.

**Do:** frame on the area in question; label areas directly; hatch what is uncertain; say in the caption what is shown, for when, from which source; use several map cards when the demonstration has several steps or several states.

**Don't:** add a paragraph under the map; use a country outline as the default frame; turn a population into a homogeneous block; infer identity from language or citizenship; draw a boundary the sources do not document. See the Maps section.

**Historical atlas map (1.2.0):** for a series of continent maps going back in time. Zone maps take `map.atlas: {entries: [{name, x, y, region, kind: "state" | "society" | "site", zone, uncertain, offset}], keys: [{region, x, y, width, columns}], keyRows}`: each entry is an approximate zone at one moment (`zone`: an ellipse or a few points, built from the documented extent, never traced; ochre for a state, teal for a society without a king, red earth for an archaeological culture; hatched when uncertain), clipped to the coast. Its name is written inside it, or just beside it; a zone too small for its name gets a 38 px numbered marker and a line in a light key over the sea under Nord, Ouest, Centre, Est, Sud. Up to 45 entries, 15 under one heading. Filled maps take `map.categorical` (1914: eight colour-safe powers, the territories of the period outlined and named with `categorical.zones`, Morocco striped) or `map.choropleth` with `map.marks` (independence: Western Sahara hatched, a ring for states never colonised), both with `borders: "dotted"`. Every map of the series carries `timeline: {steps, current, from, to}`, a row of dots in the panel head. Names with two forms are written « Danxomè (Dahomey) » in one style; « ° » marks an uncertain date, explained once under the legend.

**Atlas checks:** the fit report refuses a card when two markers are closer than 44 px, a leader is longer than 72 px, a key sits on Africa, under the gradient or over another key, a text is cut by the edge, or any two map texts, markers or boxes overlap (`report.collisions`).

**Examples:** `atlas-1880` (dense, 40 entries), `atlas-1914`, `atlas-independances`, `atlas-1500` (sparse), `atlas-1880-parchemin`, `atlas-capacite-45` (capacity test, points without zones), `atlas-proto-collisions` (must fail). See Maps, « Historical atlas map ».
