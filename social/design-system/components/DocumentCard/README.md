The document card sets an explanation on the document it discusses, with up to two zooms.

**Provide:** `type: "document"`, the fields of an explanation, `image` with its focal point on the line, title or stamp being discussed, and optional `insets: [{src, x, y, width, label}]` in card pixels.

**Do:** crop so the discussed passage is readable above the panel; label each inset in two or three words (« le royaume », « le protectorat »); credit the holder and the licence.

**Don't:** place an inset over the panel or over the passage; show offensive passages next to the useful one when a crop can avoid them; replace a real document with a reconstruction.
