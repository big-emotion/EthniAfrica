The opening card asks the question the carousel answers, with its title alone.

**Provide:** `type: "opener"`, `kicker`, `title` (a question, one `*accent*` word, up to three lines at 116 px), `cue` (« Faites défiler »), `image` with its focal point, `credit`.

**Do:** open on the name, not on an occasion; keep a greeting (an anniversary, a national day) for the caption unless the operator asks for it on the card. Put the image's subject in the upper part.

**Don't:** add a paragraph under the title; promise an answer the later cards or the site page do not give; use a stock or generated picture.
