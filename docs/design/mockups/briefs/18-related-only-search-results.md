# Brief 18 — Related-only search results

Written 2026-10-18 for ETNI-2111. Status: **approved by the operator in chat on 2026-10-18**. The approved proposal was adapted to the example « bukasa » from the source data and the `PAT_MUKASA` fiche before recording.

## State and order

When a name search has no answering entity but does find related fiches, show the filters first, then the verdict, then any appellations, then the found fiches as the primary content. Do not add the `owed` closing. Keep relation-filter browsing distinct: it retains its « Résultats liés » eyebrow.

The name-search verdict has no « D’où vient ce nom » eyebrow. The searched form remains the page title; the verdict says only that related fiches were found without establishing that they answer the name.

## Fiche block

Use the factual title « La fiche trouvée » for one fiche and « Les fiches trouvées » for several. Do not add a subtitle. The 430, 768 and 1440 px boards use « bukasa » and one fiche, Mukasa, with the short description from its fiche: « Un nom ganda, aussi celui d'un dieu du lac Victoria ; il vient peut-être de l'île de Bukasa. »

## Other unresolved states

Remove « D’où vient ce nom » from unknown-name and typo verdicts as well. Keep their searched form, verdict, choices and remaining blocks in their approved order.

## Recorded boards

`search-state-related-430`, `search-state-related-768` and `search-state-related-1440` are new. The unknown and typo boards and the corresponding `Main` examples are updated to omit the eyebrow. `Main` also records both fiche-title numbers.
