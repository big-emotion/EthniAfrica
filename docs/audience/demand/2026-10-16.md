# Reader demand journal — 2026-10-16

Fifth run of the nightly demand pass. It measures what readers search and read
in Plausible, measures how empty each demanded fiche still is in git, and
enriches the most read and emptiest ones. Consented sessions only: every figure
is a floor, never the audience. Fiches are judged **from git (`recette`)**, never
from the live site.

## Enriched in this run

**None.** No fiche was changed, and no test was written. The ladder was tried in
order:

1. **UNESCO:** unesdoc answered 403.
2. **Wikipedia:** reachable, but PAT_ABAZA already rests on the English article
   on the Abaza family, and that article gives no source for the name's
   etymology that could replace it.
3. **Scholarly sources:** the works on the Egyptian Abaza family (Marsot, Abaza)
   are restricted on archive.org. The other candidates (PAT_MAMBA, PAT_SOUMAH,
   PAT_SAWADOGO, PAT_TOURE) already rest on the sources reachable from here.

Filling a fiche from sources that could not be read would invent content, so the
fiches stay as they are and the work is carried over.
