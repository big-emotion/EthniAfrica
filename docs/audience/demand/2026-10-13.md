## Enriched in this run

Only two of the ten planned fiches were done. The ranking is by priority, and
the other eight are carried over rather than filled with weak sources.

| PAT_ABAZA  |         0.31 → 0.51 |               7 → 7 |                  1 → 1 |

**PAT_SOUMAH.** The fiche already had a name history, but the structured fields
were still filled from the coverage queue. They now rest on what is already
cited in the history, reread on 2026-10-13 at pages 281 and 298-299 of André
Arcin, _La Guinée française_ (1907): the Souma as chiefs of the central Soso,
their fief in the Gania, the Dubréka state, the supposed kinship with the Keïta,
and the Soumaré as « fils de Souma ». The Bamadaba dictionary lists Suma and
Sumaoro apart, which is recorded as a contested reading. Source ladder: (a)
UNESCO's _Histoire générale de l'Afrique_ was not opened for this name, so no
chapter is cited; (b) Wikipedia was not used; (c) one archive source and one
linguistic reference. Open: oral traditions on the name, alliances, bearers,
transmission mode, each with what was searched.

**PAT_ABAZA.** The fiche had no search and no name history. Ladder: (a) HGA not
relevant, the name comes from the Caucasus; (b) the English Wikipedia article
_Abaza family_ was read and is the only source cited, as an encyclopedia entry at
tier unverified, because the books it relies on (al-Sayyid-Marsot 1984, p. 123;
Springborg 2016; Mills 2014) are lending-library titles whose full text could not
be reached. The fiche says so, and attributes each thesis. (c) No scholarly or
Arabic source could be read. This fiche needs a second pass with those books.
Fill rose because the name history, origin and gaps are now written; the gap
count stays at 7 since each one now says what was searched.
