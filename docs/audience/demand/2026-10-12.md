# Reader demand journal — 2026-10-12

First run of the nightly demand pass. Its job is to measure what readers
search and read in Plausible. Then it measures how much of each demanded fiche
is still empty, and enriches the fiches that are both most read and emptiest,
with the name's origin, its different names and where each comes from.
Consented sessions only: every figure is a floor, never the audience.

The fiches are judged **from git (`recette`), never from the live site**,
because the two can differ (see _Awaiting deployment_).

## How the figures were collected

Plausible internal API, `https://stats.ethniafrica.com/api/stats/ethniafrica.com`,
no key, `detailed=true`, `limit=300`. Every report answered `200` in every
window.

| Report family                             | Endpoints                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| ----------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Headline                                  | `top-stats`, `current-visitors`                                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Pages                                     | `pages`, `entry-pages`, `exit-pages`                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Acquisition                               | `sources`, `channels`, `utm_mediums`, `utm_sources`, `utm_campaigns`, `utm_contents`, `utm_terms`                                                                                                                               |
| Location                                  | `countries`, `regions`, `cities`                                                                                                                                                                                                |
| Devices                                   | `browsers`, `operating-systems`, `screen-sizes`                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| Goals                                     | `conversions`                                                                                                                                                                                                                   |
| Event properties (`custom-prop-values/*`) | `search:submit` → `query`, `results`, `type`, `surface`; `search:result_click` → `type`, `rank`; `fiche:depth` → `surface`, `depth`; `fiche:related_click` → `from`, `to`; `game:start` → `game`; `report:open` → `target_type` |

Filters use the list form, for example
`filters=[["is","event:goal",["search:submit"]],["is","event:props:results",["0"]]]`
for the searches that found nothing.

Known limits of this instance:

- **`cities` is always empty.** The instance has no city geolocation base.
- **`pages` hits the 300-row cap** on 28 and 91 days, so the long tail of fiches
  read once is cut there.
- **`91d` works** here, unlike `6mo` and `12mo`, which return zero.

what readers type is therefore invisible to this pass. Fixing it is a code
change outside the corpus and needs its own ticket.

## Enriched in this run

**PAT_MUSA** (readers arrive on `/fr/atlas/noms/PAT_MOUSSA`, retired into it).
Chosen by the operator as the worked example; it ranks 31st on priority.

| Measure       | Before | After |
| ------------- | -----: | ----: |
| Fill          |   0.58 |  0.83 |
| `gaps[]`      |      7 |     5 |
| Prose (chars) |  2 163 | 4 427 |
| Sources       |      6 |     9 |

The source ladder was followed in order:

1. **UNESCO.** _Histoire générale de l'Afrique_, IV, ch. 6 (Niane, 1985),
   read in the full PDF. Kanku Mūsā, Mansa Mūsā I, reigned from 1307 to about
   1332 (p. 171). Ibn Khaldūn's twenty-five years of reign are corrected
   (p. 173, n. 62). Oral traditions rarely mention him (p. 176, n. 68).
2. **Wikipedia.** The French, English and Arabic articles were read. They give
   the Arabic form موسى, the cognate Moses and the spellings
   Mosa/Moosa/Mousa/Moussa, but cite no source for the etymology, so none of
   them is cited in the fiche.
3. **Other sources.** Arthur Jeffery, _The Foreign Vocabulary of the Qur'ān_
   (1938), p. 274-275: Arab philologists held the name to be foreign (the
   "water and trees" folk etymology, Hebrew or Coptic). Jeffery thinks a
   direct Hebrew origin is possible, but a route through Syriac or Ethiopic
   more likely, and knows no well-attested use before the Qur'an.

The fiche now starts its history with Moïse in the Hebrew Bible as what came
before. The name is born as Mūsā in the Qur'an, is borne by Mansa Mūsā I of
Mali in the fourteenth century, and is written in Hausa by Schön in 1876, who
is no longer marked as the birth. The fiche also records the Hausa homonym
mūsa, "cat", and links the Malinké as a supposed people.

Still open, each with what was searched: `bearers` (Mansa Mūsā has no
person fiche yet), `alliances`, `casteOrSocialFunction`, `origin.oralTraditions`,
and `peoples` (the Zarma and Malinké links stay supposed). Spellings by country
beyond Nigeria and Niger need an attesting source per country; Wikipedia's
spelling list gives none.
