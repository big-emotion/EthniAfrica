# Adaptation shortlist

Seven candidates drawn from already-published contents, from a possible ten.
Figures are lifetime views at 2026-09-26 (ages 1–21 days); row ids point to
[`publications.csv`](publications.csv); the reasoning is in the
[executive report](README.md).

**Revised 2026-09-29 (version 2).** Corrections to version 1:

- Candidates are **not ranked by reach**. Version 1 ordered them by "proven
  attention", which for the leading posts meant disagreement (report F7), and
  the audit's own rule is never to rank by a best post. Attention-maximising
  disputed claims are not selected as editorial truth here.
- "Only production, not research" was wrong for Lingala and Dioula. Readers
  raised counter-accounts on both, so both need a source review first.
- The four "not recommended" subjects were not exhausted by having editions.
  They are reprioritised below, and none is excluded.
- A post published on 25 Sep is judged at 7 days on 2 Oct, not on 28 Sep.
- Lingala's first video sat at 24 views on a day-one account; the claim that
  the other Lingala videos "predate the audience" is dropped, because the file
  does not record the audience on that day.
- No review policy is restated. Each candidate goes through whatever the
  production chain currently requires for the claims and media it actually
  contains; that is decided when the piece is planned.

Three different actions stay apart, and none is required for any subject:

- **Adapt**: same angle, other format, same or other network.
- **Deepen**: a new piece that answers what readers raised about the old one.
- **Republish**: the same piece again, because its first showing was not a
  fair test.

A published edition never closes a subject or an angle. This is a proposal,
not permission to produce or publish.

## Sequencing considerations

The seven candidates are not ordered by expected reach. What sequences them is
outstanding work:

1. **Ready for a source review now:** 1, 2, 5, 6. All four carry a dispute
   readers already voiced, so the review comes before any script.
2. **Ready to plan as a comparison:** 3 and 4, run under the counterbalanced
   design in [`validation-plan.md`](validation-plan.md), after their source
   review.
3. **Needs the operator's editorial choice:** 7, and which of the reprioritised
   subjects to reopen and for what purpose.

Whether any of them is produced, and in which format, remains the operator's
decision.
