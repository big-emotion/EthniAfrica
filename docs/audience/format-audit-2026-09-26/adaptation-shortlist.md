# Adaptation shortlist

Seven already-published contents worth acting on, from a possible ten. The
evidence did not support more, so none was added to reach the number. Figures
are lifetime views at 2026-09-26 (ages 1–21 days); row ids point to
[`publications.csv`](publications.csv); the reasoning is in the
[executive report](README.md).

Three different actions are kept apart:

- **Adapt**: same angle, other format, same or other network.
- **Deepen**: a new piece that answers what readers raised about the old one.
- **Republish**: the same piece again, because its first showing was not a
  fair test.

**Preconditions for every item.** This is a proposal, not permission to
produce or publish. Every candidate goes back through the usual chain (idea,
structure, the myth check, the message gate, the reader-facing register)
before any card is written. Where readers disputed a claim, the fiche and its
sources are re-read first; a commenter's counter-claim is a lead, never a
ruling, and both accounts are named at their own tier.

## Considered and not recommended

- **Keïta and Coulibaly as a carousel.** The reel is our largest Instagram
  result (16,084 views, 385 saves, 453 followers). A carousel already exists
  twice, on TikTok (181) and as an Instagram+Facebook cross-post (350
  combined). More carousels are not supported by this evidence.
- **Fulbe's four names.** Both formats exist on TikTok; the carousel led 4.6×
  but was posted three days earlier. Nothing left to adapt.
- **Krou.** Strong as a reel on TikTok (1,309), Instagram (5,069) and
  Facebook (2,747). No reason found for another format.
- **Ghana, "the empire that was never there".** Our best YouTube Short
  (1,518 views, 96% average viewed) already ran on TikTok (418) and as an
  Instagram+Facebook cross-post (1,130 combined). Every network is covered.
- **Anything published 21–25 September** (the frontières trio, Mali, the
  griot and ethnie pieces, the Ghana series, Goma, Mbappé, the introduction).
  Too recent: judge at 7 days, no earlier than 28 September.
