# Carousel versus reel: decision matrix

Companion to the [executive report](README.md). Each row is an editorial
approach, judged on what the reader needs to do with it, then on our own
evidence. Figures are lifetime views at collection (2026-09-26), ages 1–21
days; row ids refer to [`publications.csv`](publications.csv).

**How to read the "evidence" column.** _Observed_ is a measured result on a
named publication. _Suggestive_ means the pattern appears but subject, date or
network is not controlled. _None_ means we published nothing comparable.
Nothing below is a rule; each row ends in the uncertainty that would change it.

## Rules of thumb that our evidence supports

1. **On Instagram, choose reels for discovery.** Median 1,274 views for reels
   against 141 for carousels; one same-day pair (swahili) is 30×. A carousel
   there works as a companion, not as the first exposure.
2. **On Facebook, publish reels.** Our 11 multi-photo posts drew 3–30 views.
   All three Facebook results above 60K were reels. This is about our page,
   not the platform.
3. **On TikTok, format is not the main lever.** Medians are equal. What differs
   is subject, and the carousel's right tail (five posts above 2,000 views).
   Choose the format by the reading task, then judge it on the first 7 days.
4. **YouTube: Shorts only.** 30 Shorts, median 532; seven community image posts
   drew 5–21 views. Use community posts as a pointer, not as a carousel venue.
5. **LinkedIn and X did not test formats.** The 12 LinkedIn videos with
   metrics have a median of 2 views; the best (a 22 Sep introduction) has 51 (124
   impressions). X has 0 followers. No format conclusion is possible.

## Decision questions to ask before choosing

- _What must the reader do with it?_ Compare, check, save → carousel becomes
  credible. Follow a sequence, hear, be surprised → reel.
- _Is the claim disputed by the people it names?_ If yes, expect a reel to
  carry the argument and a carousel to carry the sources; plan the source
  check first, as the comments on Lingala, Dioula, swahili and Mandé already
  show.
- _Which network is it for?_ The same video was 12.6× the Instagram median and
  0.4× the Facebook median (Keïta): choose subject × network, not format alone.
- _Is one format a copy of the other?_ Do not republish a subject as a
  carousel only because the reel worked; a republication needs its own job.
