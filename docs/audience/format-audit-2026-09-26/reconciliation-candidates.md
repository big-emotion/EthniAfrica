## What this list does not do

It does not decide which record is right, backfill an observation, or
reconstruct a publication date. Observations for any occurrence belong to the
[measurement protocol](measurement-protocol.md): a lifetime total from
2026-09-26 is one observation with an age, not a 7-day or 28-day one.
