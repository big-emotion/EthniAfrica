## Handoffs

### For /ethniafrica-experience-optimizer

- **Mercator: six reports, one verdict, zero changes.** 81 % bounce, high
  time, high scroll — a page that is read and then abandoned. This is now the
  longest-standing unresolved finding in the series. Its traffic mix should be
  checked once more before redesigning (it was LinkedIn-dominated at the first
  reading); if that mix has shifted toward Direct/social, the page itself is
  the more likely fix, not the channel.
- **`/fr/about` needs an onward step**, unchanged from the previous handoff:
  67 % bounce after an 83 %-scroll read. It is the landing page the doctrine
  correction points readers to, and it currently ends every session it starts.
- **Two Facebook reels this period reached over 75 K views each and sent a
  combined 6 tagged link clicks.** Before any site-side design work, check
  whether the caption links actually resolve with a visible, tappable URL on
  mobile Facebook — the site cannot fix a click that never happens upstream.
- **`FLG_MANDE` (the linguistic-family fiche) is now the subject of the two
  biggest Facebook reels the project has produced.** It holds the site's
  longest average reads (205s) and is not a dead end (33 % bounce). It is the
  one fiche in this report's page-verdicts table that is both high-reach-adjacent
  and functioning — worth understanding why, as a template for other fiches.
- **`PAT_TRAORE` and `PAT_DIARRA` keep their five-report pattern**: 100 %
  bounce on both, but partial (33–77 %) rather than full scroll — narrower
  dead ends than Mercator or `/about`. A next-name suggestion at the bottom of
  a name fiche is still untested.
- **Mobile duration gap widened to 3.8×** (was flagged as "watch" last
  report at a smaller gap). This crosses from noise into a pattern worth the
  art director's attention at 430 px.

### For /ethniafrica-content-strategist

- **Reach at this scale (75 K+ per reel, twice this period) is not
  converting to measurable site visits — the pipeline problem is now clearly
  upstream of the site.** Before planning new subjects, the highest-leverage
  open question is why a Facebook reel's own caption link produces 3 clicks
  on 75 000 views. This is not a content question.
- **Patronymes are the corpus's least-visited class and the least-served by
  the publishing plan simultaneously.** Two patronyme-adjacent posts went out
  this period (Keïta/Coulibaly pacte, Diallo carousel) and neither moved the
  44-fiche visited count. Worth checking whether either post's caption links
  to a patronyme fiche at all, or only to the corresponding people/family page.
- **TikTok needs a new post.** Weekly views fell 19 % with the account idle
  since ~09-16; a channel that stops publishing on a platform driven entirely
  by the algorithmic feed (95 % "Pour toi") loses momentum fastest there.
- **`FLG_MANDE` is the clearest evidence yet that a linguistic-family subject
  can be the reach champion**, not just a people or country subject. Two
  different cuts of the same subject (a straight explainer and a longer recut)
  both performed — worth deliberately testing another family-level subject.
- **X should be stopped or restarted deliberately — third report running
  with this exact recommendation, unactioned.** 0 followers, single-digit
  views per post, no analytics without Premium.
- **Pipeline state**, for reference: 95 posts across 74 subjects, **35
