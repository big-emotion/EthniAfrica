## Findings

1. Google brought 43 visitors in the last week against 17 the week before, and
   with ChatGPT (12) accounts for 55 of the week's 92 visitors: search, not the
   networks, is what grew the site.
2. Network views hit their highest level (Facebook 403.5K / 28 d, Instagram
   214.7K / 30 d, TikTok 27.6K / 7 d), while the networks sent 9 consented site
   visitors in the same week (Instagram 7, Facebook 2, LinkedIn 0).
3. The Guinée reel reached 115,774 Meta views with 0 link clicks, no
   `utm_campaign` row and 2 visits on `/fr/atlas/pays/GIN`; the pipeline also
   lists that subject as never published.
4. The « race » reel drew the most shares (2,209) and saves (1,901) of any post,
   288 comments, and has no ledger file or campaign: the best-performing piece
   of the period is the one the measurement chain cannot join.
5. Seventeen of the twenty TikTok posts of 1–3 October sat below 100 views at
   collection; the corporations carousel (517) and Kora Awards (273 in 3 h) are
   the exceptions. The cause cannot be isolated from this data.
6. Instagram recorded 107 external link taps against 43 consented Instagram
   visitors in Plausible over 30 days, a lower bound on consent loss for that
   channel.
7. The six sampled TikTok comments contain four contributed accounts of a name's
   origin and no answer to the posts' calls to action.
8. Mercator (76% bounce, 17 entries), About (67%, 89% scroll) and the Mandé
   family fiche (235 s, exit rate 77.8%) remain dead ends: read, then left.
9. 33 visited `decouvertes/*` pages are missing from the sitemap, and their
   visits last 2–15 seconds at 100% scroll.
10. YouTube had 80 views in the last 48 hours and a near-flat curve since
    ~26 September, although the Studio chart still marks uploads around 27 and 30 September.

## Handoffs

### For /ethniafrica-experience-optimizer

- **Add the Découvertes publications to the sitemap.** `src/app/sitemap.ts`
  omits `decouvertes/[[...publication]]`; 33 of those pages were visited this
  month and Google is now the first source. Test-first: a sitemap test that
  asserts a known publication slug is listed.
- **Read a `decouvertes/*` page at 430 px first.** 2–15 seconds at 100% scroll
  on every visited one suggests a page that is all seen at once and offers
  nothing to do next. Check what follows the publication, then tablet, then desktop.
- **Three dead ends, ordered by entries:** Mercator (17 entry visits, 76%
  bounce, unchanged since 09-07), `FLG_MANDE` (235 s then 77.8% exit),
  `/fr/about` (89% scroll then 67% bounce). Each needs one onward step to a
  name, not a new section.
- **Instrument `fiche:source_click`** (declared, never emitted). Until it fires,
  "do readers open the sources" has no answer.

### For /ethniafrica-content-strategist

- **Search is now the growth channel; the networks are reach without visits.**
  A week of record views produced 9 consented visits from the networks and
  55 from Google and ChatGPT. Plan site work (fiches people search for) and
  social work as two different jobs, and stop counting on posts to feed the site.
- **Tag every link before the next post.** None of the 1–3 October posts
  carries a campaign, nor does the Guinée reel. Without `utm_campaign` a 115K-view
  reel and a 9-view carousel look the same on the site side.
- **Reconcile two ledgers before reusing the period's two leaders as
  comparables**: the Guinée reel (`guinee-vingt-neuf-peuples`, listed as never
  published by `bilan-sujets`) and the « race » reel (no ledger file). A
  publication the ledger does not know cannot anchor a plan.
- **Test the publishing volume, one variable at a time.** About twenty TikTok
  posts in three days; seventeen below 100 views at collection. Compare a day
  with one post against a day with several, same format and hour, before
  concluding anything about cadence.
- **Answer the Lingala commenters.** Four of six comments bring an origin
  account. A reply that thanks, attributes each reading to its bearer and crowns
  none is both the doctrine and the invitation to contribute; the ones that
  arrive with a source belong in the corpus at `unverified`, with provenance.
- **Read before the next run**: the comment text of the « race » reel
  (288 comments) and of the unidentified 2026-09-17 reel (416 comments on 860
  reactions), plus the 3 October posts again once they are 72 hours old.
