### Signals of understanding

- **YouTube: none.** Zero comments across 16 Shorts and ~9.8 K views. Retention
  is the only signal, and it separates two groups: the September 5–9 Shorts hold
  63–97 % of their length, the September 12 batch 53–67 %.
- **TikTok: the Dioula carousel is contested on its central claim.** Of the
  comments read, one says outright that _djoula_ does not mean merchant in
  Mandinka and gives two other words for merchant; a second proposes a Soninké
  origin for the word; a third — the most liked — adds that most lineages
  calling themselves Dioula descend from Wangara, Soninké and Hausa traders, and
  that another set descends from Malinké marabout lineages. The message was
  understood and argued with, which is the atlas's stated invitation — and it is
  now a claim readers dispute in public.
- **TikTok: identification.** A comment on the Akan carousel names the
  commenter's own Agni town. This is the reaction the "Quel est ton peuple ?"
  prompt asks for.
- **TikTok: one misreading.** A comment on the Amazigh carousel reads the post as
  claiming Algeria belongs to one group, and calls for unity. The post says
  nothing of the kind; the misreading is itself the signal.
- **Facebook: no conversation started.** 113 interactions over 28 days. The
  recent-content list shows unlabelled figures per post, so what those
  interactions were — reactions, comments, shares — was not read.
- **Instagram: not read.** Interactions are counted (1,482); what the comments
  say is not exposed on the insights page.

## Findings

1. Site visitors per day doubled (12.6 → 24) and bounce fell from 49 % to 10 %, but 56 of the 72 visitors arrived on a single day, 09-13.
2. Instagram went from 1 visitor to 28, 26 of them on 09-13, all through the profile's bio link, bouncing 4 % — the first network to send measurable traffic.
3. Instagram visitors left from six patronyme fiches matching the Mali name posts, and name fiches (28 visitors) overtook people fiches (22) on `fiche:depth`.
4. LinkedIn fell from 34 visitors to 0 while posts went out on it on 09-11 and 09-12; its studio was not read.
5. No post published since 09-11 produced a scheme-tagged visit; the only campaign ever recorded is still `traore-diop`, with 1 visitor.
6. The four networks together report tens of thousands of views, ≥ 94 % from non-followers on Instagram and Facebook, against 30 site visitors attributed to them since collection began (Instagram 28, Facebook 1, YouTube 1).
7. Search became the site's main tool: 51 of 72 visitors submitted 120 queries, 7 of them got zero results, and the search page is the first exit page (22 visitors).
8. `fiche:related_click` fires in production (6 visitors, 13 % of fiche readers), closing the previous report's open question.
9. Fiche readers reaching 75 % rose from a third to two thirds, but only 7 of 46 reach 100 %.
10. `PAT_TRAORE` bounced every one of its 5 visitors across the collection, in two consecutive reports.
11. YouTube's 16 Shorts carry zero comments; the 09-12 batch holds 53–67 % of its length against 63–97 % for the 09-05 → 09-09 Shorts.
12. On TikTok the Dioula carousel's central claim — _jula_ means merchant — is contested by at least two commenters, one giving alternative words.
13. Three of the five search queries bringing people to the TikTok account are about the true-size-of-Africa map vote.
14. France rose from 45 % to 82 % of site visitors; at least 4 of 72 were in African countries, against 44 of 101.
15. Fiche coverage rose from 82 to 151 URLs (9.0 % of 1 684), driven by patronymes (12 → 38).

## Method

Site figures come from the instance's unauthenticated internal JSON API with
`detailed=true` and explicit limits: `top-stats`, `pages` (limit 500, so the
coverage is untruncated), `entry-pages`, `exit-pages`, `sources`,
`screen-sizes`, `countries`, `conversions`, `utm_sources`, `utm_mediums`,
`utm_campaigns`, `utm_contents`, `referrers/Instagram`, `main-graph` for daily
curves, and `custom-prop-values/{depth,surface,results}` filtered by goal.
Per-source breakdowns use `filters=[["is","visit:source",["<source>"]]]`. The
previous window was re-queried and reproduces the 2026-09-12 report's 101
visitors, 120 visits and 350 pageviews exactly.

Networks were read in the operator's logged-in browser, read only: YouTube
Studio dashboard, Shorts list and Analytics overview; TikTok Studio
`analytics/overview`, `analytics/content` and the comments page; Instagram
`accounts/insights/?timeframe=30`; Meta Business Suite Insights overview. No
account identifier is recorded here. LinkedIn was not read.

Release times from `gh release list`; release contents from `gh release view`.
Post publication dates from `social/tools/etat-pipeline/bilan-sujets.mjs`. Corpus
counts from `find dataset/source/afrik -name '*.json'`.
