## Findings

1. Search and AI answers brought 48 of the week's 81 consented visitors; ChatGPT
   rose from 10 to 15, the networks together brought 7.
2. Networks keep growing on Meta (Facebook 459.6K views over 28 days, 91.9%
   non-followers; Instagram 247.3K over 30 days, 2,896 followers) while TikTok's
   seven days fell 36.2% to 15.5K and its profile views 51.9%.
3. `search:result_click` read 4 for 79 searches this week (~5%); the answer
   blocks still emit no event, unchanged since 10-07.
4. Instagram counts 114 external link taps against 44 consented Instagram
   visitors over 30 days, still the clearest lower bound on consent loss.
5. YouTube appears as a site source for the first time (2 visitors), while its
   28-day views fell from 18.2K to 15.9K and the latest Short (« Soudan »)
   drew 4 views in two days.
6. Four of TikTok's five recorded search queries ask where a name comes from
   (lingala, Soninké, Mandingue/Diola, Nzebi).
7. Dead ends persist on `/fr/atlas/pays/CIV` (100% bounce, 73% scroll, 107s),
   `FLG_MANDE` (exit 75%) and `/fr/about` (86% scroll, 67% bounce).
8. No post published since 1 October carries a campaign, and X has had no
   post since 16 September.

## Handoffs

### For /ethniafrica-experience-optimizer

- **Instrument the answer blocks before redesigning the result page**, carried
  unchanged from 10-07. Test-first: a component test asserting that following
  a link under `src/components/search/answer/` fires `search:result_click`
  with `surface: "answer"`, distinct from the list rows in
  `src/components/pages/RecherchePageContent.tsx`. This matters more now that
  a timeline result page is being considered
  (`docs/editorial/doctrine.md`, §1): without the
  event, neither the current page nor its successor can be measured.
- **Add `decouvertes/*` to `src/app/sitemap.ts`** (41 visited pages, Google is
  the second source). Test-first: a sitemap test asserting a known
  publication slug is listed.
- **Dead ends, ordered by severity:** `/fr/atlas/pays/CIV` (everyone leaves
  after reading 73%), `FLG_MANDE` (6 entries, exit 75%), `/fr/about` (86%
  scroll, 67% bounce). Each needs one onward step to a name. Côte d'Ivoire is
  the second visiting country, which makes the CIV page the most costly.
- `/fr/atlas/peuples` stays at 50% bounce at 73% scroll: a directory read to
  the bottom without a next step.

### For /ethniafrica-content-strategist

- **Tag every post that links to the site.** No post since 1 October appears in
  `utm_campaign`; the only post campaign with visits is Bouët-Willaumez (4).
  Until links are tagged, no network post can be credited with a site visit.
- **Search engines and AI assistants are the site's channel.** 48 of 81 weekly
  visitors; ChatGPT +50% in a week. Each published subject needs its answer
  page indexable, which the Découvertes sitemap gap currently prevents for 41
  visited pages.
- **TikTok is contracting** (−36.2% views, −51.9% profile views over 7 days)
  while Meta holds. The 10-07 report's cadence hypothesis (volume diluting
  distribution) is still unproven; this week's drop does not choose between
  cadence, subject and hour.
- **Name-origin questions are what TikTok search brings**: lingala, Soninké,
  Mandingue/Diola, Nzebi. These are candidate subjects whose answer pages
  should exist before the post.
- **YouTube's last Short stalled at 4 views in two days**, while the reply-to-a-
  comment piece (« La Guinée a-t-elle été la première ? ») still leads the
  channel at 178. The 10-07 report's single closed loop remains the one case.
