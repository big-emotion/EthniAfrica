figures below are **16 days of data, not thirty**. "Collection to date" means

## Read this first

- **One reel now holds more than a third of the project's Facebook reach, and
  nothing on the site moved.** A Facebook reel published 09-17 (18 s, its card
  reads « … la carte ne montrait que des routes », title only partly legible
  from the thumbnail) took **75 677 views, 52 438 viewers, 496 new followers and
  400 comments** — 38.7 % of the page's 195.3 K views over 28 days. Facebook as a
  whole recorded **5 link clicks** in that window and Plausible attributes **9
  visitors** to Facebook since 09-04. The reach is real; the path to the site is
  not being taken.
- **The site's own three-day window is flat on volume and worse on quality.**
  43 unique visitors against 43 in the previous short window, but bounce went
  from 15 % to **33 %**, views per visit from 4.07 to 3.20 and visit duration
  from 4m 46s to **2m 23s**. This is the first measured degradation in the
  series, and it coincides with the largest reach event the project has had.
- **The consent floor holds at the same ratio on Instagram.** 81 external link
  taps over 30 days against 35 attributed Instagram visitors is **43 %**, the
  same 43 % as last report's 77 taps against 33 visitors. Two independent
  readings now agree on order of magnitude: roughly four in ten Instagram
  arrivals appear in Plausible.
- **Mercator's "dead end" is mostly one LinkedIn post.** 15 of its 21 visitors
  came from LinkedIn, at 80 % bounce, from what is very likely the operator's own
  post (385 impressions, it links the page). It is a finding about that audience's expectation, not
  evidence about the page across channels.
- **`/fr/atlas/recherche` is still the product's working surface.** 102
  visitors, 7 % bounce, and 32 of Instagram's 35 visitors go from the home to
  it. The home-to-search hand-off works; what is unread is what search returns.
- **LinkedIn was read this run, partially.** Through the operator's own
  activity feed only: company-page analytics were not reachable (see the end).
