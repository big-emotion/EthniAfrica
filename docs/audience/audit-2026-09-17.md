# Audience audit — 2026-09-17

Period measured, site: the Plausible instance's `30d` window returns
**2026-08-18 → 2026-09-16**, but collection only began on **2026-09-04**, so the
figures below are **13 days of data, not thirty**. Where this report says
"collection to date" it means 09-04 → 09-16. A second window,
**09-15 → 09-17**, is measured separately to compare against the previous report
(which covered 09-12 → 09-14). Networks: each studio's own window, stated per
network, read in the operator's logged-in browser on 09-17.

**These are consented sessions only.** Plausible loads after the consent banner
is accepted, so every site figure is a floor, not the audience. At this volume
rank and ratio are the signal; absolute counts are not.

## Read this first

- **The site is the smallest surface the project occupies, by three orders of
  magnitude.** Facebook served 91 200 views in 28 days, Instagram 49 444 in 30,
  TikTok 35 900 in 7, YouTube 15 486 in 28. The site recorded 205 consented
  visitors in 13 days. Never add the network figures into one total — their
  windows differ — but the gap does not depend on the arithmetic.
- **The consent floor now has an empirical bound, for one channel.** Instagram
  counts **77 external link taps** over 30 days; Plausible attributes **33
  Instagram visitors** over the overlapping period. If a tap is roughly a
  session, the consented sample is on the order of **40–45 % of arrivals** from
  that channel. The windows are not identical and a tap is not a session, so
  treat this as an order of magnitude, not a rate. It is still the first
  measured constraint on how deep the floor is.
- **An editorial incident falls inside this window.** The Lingala carousel
  (« Le lingala, une langue inventée par les Belges ? », published 09-16 on
  TikTok, Instagram and LinkedIn) was read as an assertion rather than a
  question, and drew the most hostile comments the project has received. On
  09-17 the operator published a doctrine clarification across Facebook,
  Instagram and TikTok. This is the first measured case of a hook form
  misfiring, and it is documented under Networks below.
- **`chatgpt.com` is now the third acquisition source** over 09-15 → 09-17, at 8
  of 43 visitors. With `copilot.com` and Bing, assistant referrals are a real
  channel nobody planned. They were noise in the previous two reports.
- **38 posts have been published; 2 tagged visits exist.** Campaign attribution
  is, for practical purposes, still not implemented.

### Signals of understanding

**TikTok's 100 comments in 7 days are the richest signal the project has ever
had, and most of them are about one post.**

The Lingala carousel's title — « Le lingala, une langue inventée par les
Belges ? » — was read as a claim, not a question, and rejected as such:

- « comment un belge peut inventer une langue qu'il n'est peut parler soyons
  sérieux lingala fait partie de nos langue africaine »
- « Il a osé dire inventer par les belges 🤣🤣🤣 »
- « bon on va pas se chercher les poux ds la tête je vous dis directe les noirs
  resteront tjrs ignorant si leur professeur est blanc »

Two comments engage the substance instead, and both are worth routing to
curation:

- **« boloki et non bobangi »** — 5 likes, 2 replies. A direct challenge to the
  corpus datum the post rests on.
- A competing etymology, attributed and dated: the name as Italian explorers'
  « lingua de Ngala », the Ngala as river traders, Mobutu's role in its
  nationalisation.
- A reader asking why Lingala carries Latin- and Portuguese-looking loanwords
  (« mesa », « vino », « copo », « sapato »).

The operator answered on 09-17 with a pinned clarification published across
Facebook, Instagram and TikTok, whose operative sentence is: « Quand un titre
pose une question, c'est une question — pas une réponse. » Its Facebook reel
took 539 views and 215 three-second views; its Instagram reel 197 views. **Zero
comments on either.** The correction reaches roughly 1 % of the audience the
post that caused it reached.

TikTok also exposes the **search queries that reach the account** — the only
place any platform says what people were looking for when they found the
project: « vote onu carte du monde » 20.8 %, « afrique » 12.5 %, **« dioula
n'est pas une ethnie » 8.3 %**, « geurre alliance pouvoirs comprendre la
géopolitique en 60seconde » 4.2 %, « haïtien au Bénin vodou » 4.2 %. The third
is a reader searching for a claim the project made, by its wording. TikTok
attributes only 0.1 % of its own traffic to search and 94.2 % to the For You
feed, so these are small absolute numbers describing a real intent.

Message quality is scored by `/ethniafrica-message`, not here. This section
measures reception, and the two must stay side by side: the Lingala post
performed well on reach and failed on reception.

## Findings

1. The site recorded 205 consented visitors in 13 days while the smallest of the
   four networks, YouTube, served 15 486 views in 28 days and the largest,
   Facebook, 91 200 — the site is a rounding error on the project's reach
   whichever network it is set against, and the windows differ too much to add
   them.
2. Instagram's 77 external link taps against Plausible's 33 attributed visitors
   put the consented sample at roughly 40–45 % of arrivals for that channel, the
   first empirical bound on the floor.
3. Volume fell 40 % against the previous three-day window (72 → 43 visitors)
   while bounce stayed at 15 % and duration at 4m 46s — a spike ended, the
   surface did not degrade.
4. The mobile bounce rate fell from 65 % at the 09-07 baseline to 31 %, closing
   a four-fold gap with desktop to seven points; the duration gap did not move,
   with desktop sessions still running four times longer.
5. `chatgpt.com` became the third acquisition source at 8 of 43 visitors over
   09-15 → 09-17, at 12 % bounce and 4m 14s; with `copilot.com` and Bing,
   assistant referrals are an unplanned and unattributable channel.
6. Two tagged visits exist against 38 published posts, and 33 of the 35 tagged
   visitors carry parameters Instagram's app wrote, not the link-builder's — the
   post-to-visit join still does not work.
7. TikTok served 35 900 views in seven days, up 1 065 % week on week, and has
   sent zero site visitors since collection began; its in-app browser strips the
   referrer, so untagged links make this impossible to distinguish from a
   channel that genuinely converts nobody.
8. The Lingala carousel's interrogative title was read as an assertion and drew
   the project's first hostile comment wave, forcing a doctrine clarification on
   09-17 that reached roughly 1 % of the audience the original post reached.
9. One commenter contests the corpus datum under that post — « boloki et non
   bobangi » — and another supplies a competing, attributable etymology; neither
   has been ruled on.
10. TikTok's search queries include « dioula n'est pas une ethnie », a reader
    searching for a project claim by its exact wording — evidence that a post's
    sentence can become a search term.
11. `/fr/atlas/recherche` is the site's second page (87 visitors) and its second
    exit page (28 exits), reached by 74 visitors who did not enter on it: the
    home sends readers to search, and search is where sessions end.
12. `/fr/jeux/mercator` has been a textbook dead end in all three reports —

## Handoffs

### For /ethniafrica-experience-optimizer

- **`/fr/atlas/recherche` is the priority, not Mercator.** 87 visitors reach it,
  74 of them from `/fr`, and 28 end their session there — more than any page but
  the home. A reader who searches and leaves either found nothing or found a
  result that did not invite a next click. **The `search_query_log` table
  (migration `050`) records every query and its result count and has never been
  read.** Zero-result queries are the exact evidence needed here, and reading
  them requires a production service-role key this session did not have. Get
  that read before redesigning anything.
- **`/fr/jeux/mercator`, third report running**: 81 % bounce, 96 seconds read,
  69 % scrolled. The page works and then ends. It needs one onward step from the
  game to the atlas — the game is about map distortion and the atlas holds 54
  country fiches with real geometry.
- **`/fr/atlas/noms/PAT_TRAORE`**: 100 % bounce over 6 visitors and 80 seconds
  on page, with 4 exits. The most-visited patronyme fiche ends every session it

  readers before they start. Worth opening at 430 px before assuming it is a

### For /ethniafrica-content-strategist

- **Countries convert, patronymes do not.** Country fiches are at 48 % coverage,
  patronymes at 5.4 % against 796 published. The social posts that name countries
  (Côte d'Ivoire, Liberia, Niger, Bénin, Ghana) are the ones whose fiches get
  visited. If patronyme work is to pay off, a format has to point at it —
  `PAT_TRAORE`, `PAT_DIARRA`, `PAT_COULIBALY`, `PAT_SISSOKO` and `PAT_DIALLO`
  all take visitors and all end sessions.
- **The retention evidence, from the only network that exposes it.** YouTube
  average-percentage-viewed: « Le Ghana porte le nom d'un empire qui n'était pas
  là » **96.5 %**, « D'où vient le nom Afrique » 82.1 %, « Mungo Park » 75.1 %,
  « Nigeria » 73.8 %, « Nzebi » 69.5 %, « Mandé » 64.2 %, « Sénégal » 64.2 %,
  « Mami Wata » 63.0 %, « Appolo/Nzema » 60.9 %, **« Keïta et Coulibaly » 55.7 %**.
  The Keïta post is the reach champion (14 400 views on Instagram) and the worst
  retainer on the list. Reach and retention are ranking different things here,
  and a plan built on views alone would pick the wrong model.
- **The « empire qui n'était pas là » shape holds 96.5 %.** A named entity, a
  flat contradiction, a short runtime (0:33). That is the most-retained thing
  the project has made.
- **TikTok is the growth surface and the blind one.** 35 900 views in 7 days,
  +1 065 %, still rising on the last day measured, 100 comments, 277 shares —
  and zero measurable site visits, because the links are untagged and its in-app
  browser strips referrers. Tagging TikTok links is the cheapest measurement win
  available.
- **Facebook is the largest surface and the least understood.** 91 200 views,
  93.1 % non-followers, +3 382 net follows in 28 days against YouTube's +120 in
  the same window, with 54.5 % of views lasting 3 seconds. Instagram's new
  followers were not read, only its total of 1 104, so the two cannot be
  compared. No per-post reading was taken this run.
- **The Lingala reception is a planning input, not just an incident.** An
  interrogative headline over a contested claim was read as an assertion by the
  audience it was written for. The corpus doctrine already says assertion must
  track certainty; this measures the case where the sentence was correctly
  hedged and the _format_ — a headline card — asserted anyway. Any subject where
  the corpus holds competing accounts should be planned with this in mind, and
  `/ethniafrica-onomastique` and `/ethniafrica-message` should both see the post
  before the next one of its kind ships.
- **A correction reaches 1 % of what the error reached.** The 09-17 clarification
  took 539 views on Facebook and 197 on Instagram against the original's
  thousands. Budget for this asymmetry rather than assuming a follow-up repairs
  a misreading.
- **Two reader corrections are outstanding** and belong to `/afrik-curator`, not

- **Assistant referrals want a decision.** `chatgpt.com` is the third source over

- **X should be stopped or restarted deliberately.** 10 posts, 0 followers, 7–12

- **Pipeline state, for reference**: 84 posts across 69 subjects — 38 published,
