## Handoffs

### For /ethniafrica-experience-optimizer

- **Instrument the timeline before judging it.** Carried from 10-07 and now
  urgent: v5.0.0 made the timeline the result page, and the only click event is
  still in `src/components/pages/RecherchePageContent.tsx`. Test-first: a
  component test asserting that following a link from the timeline fires
  `search:result_click` with a `surface` distinct from the list rows. Without
  it the 34% bounce cannot be split between "served in place" and "left".
- **Read the result page at 430 px for a repeat-search loop.** 137 searches by
  36 visitors in the last week (3.8 each) with 13 clicks: either readers
  explore, or they do not find the answer and retry. Check whether a query
  that matches no subject offers a next step. Hypothesis, not a measured
  defect.
- **Add `decouvertes/*` to `src/app/sitemap.ts`** (41 visited pages, Google is
  the second source). Test-first: a sitemap test asserting a known publication
  slug is listed. Carried unchanged.
- **Dead ends, ordered by severity:** `/fr/atlas/pays/CIV` (Côte d'Ivoire is
  the second visiting country, everyone leaves after reading 73%),
  `FLG_MANDE` (6 entries, exit 75%), `/fr/about` (86% scroll, 67% bounce),
  `PPL_MUCUVAL` (4 entries, exit 80%). Each needs one onward step to a name.
- **`PPL_ZARMA` is a bounce-through**: 6 of 7 visitors enter there and leave
  after a third of the page. Check what the entry link promised against the
  page's first screen on mobile.

### For /ethniafrica-content-strategist

- **Tag every post that links to the site.** No post since 1 October appears in
  `utm_campaign`. « Sauvage » (196K views, the largest of the series) carried a
  bare `ethniafrica.com`; « race », « Soudan » and « Guinée première » have no
  ledger slug at all. Until links are tagged and ledgered, no network success
  can be credited with a site visit.
- **Etymology framing around a third-party clip is the strongest format
  measured so far** on Meta: « Sauvage » (196K, 3,468 shares) and « race »
  (111K, 321 comments) both wrap a translated clip in a sourced word history.
  Own productions lead on depth: the Guinée reel has the most 3-second views
  (75K) and follows (929). Two posts are not a rule; the subject, the clip
  and the hour are confounded.
- **Network reach is not reaching the site.** Meta's best week sent 3 consented
  visitors. The click-through question (what the caption asks for, where the
  link sits) matters more now than more volume.
- **TikTok and YouTube are contracting** (TikTok −31.6% in 7 days, YouTube
  9.1K from 15.9K over 28 days) while the « Lingala » and « Jula » pieces keep
  earning views a month after publication. Evergreen name questions outlast
  the week's posts.
- **Search and AI answers stay the site's channel** (46 of 105 weekly
  visitors). Each published subject needs its answer page indexable; the
  Découvertes sitemap gap still blocks 41 visited pages.
- **X has had no post for four weeks; LinkedIn sends nobody.** Either schedule
  them or record them as paused, so the next audit stops reporting their
  absence as a finding.
