### Signals of understanding

Selection: **12 top-level comments visible on the TikTok Studio comments page**,
read September 30; relative timestamps from three hours to three days. Replies
were not expanded. This is a recent convenience sample, not representative of
264 comments in the overview or of silent viewers. No personal identifiers are
retained here.

- Two dismissive reactions on the race/Lingala pieces: disagreement without
  reasoning, not enough to diagnose comprehension or decide truth.
- Two responses on Lingala and Mali address a premise of the post (colonial
  “invention”, earlier history of Mali). They suggest the question/chronology
  may be misunderstood, but a reply or comprehension check is needed to tell.
- One response discusses European naming as a practical convention: an
  alternative interpretation, not automatically misunderstanding.
- Two contributions describe cultural belonging and a journey understood as a
  visit rather than a “return”. They support leaving readers room to name their
  own relationships; neither represents a whole community or diaspora.
- Five other comments are thanks, emoji, a cultural reference, or thanks plus a
  broad inference about the name Africa. They do not demonstrate accurate recall
  of the claim and its limits.

Thus the sample has 12 reviewed comments, but **no measured comprehension rate**.
The seven-day TikTok search panel also lists “pourquoi le Mali s'appelle Mali”
and “les apollo côte d'ivoire”, each 0.6% of search traffic. The 28-day panel lists
“swahili sanskrit” and “etymologie”, each 0.3%. Queries and views are interest
signals, not proof of what the reader believes. “For You” is a traffic source,
not a non-follower percentage.

## Findings

1. Site exploration is measured (106 visitors searched; 62 opened results), but
   specific source consultation and a completed contribution are not measured here.
2. Instagram and LinkedIn each account for 40 site visitors with very different
   bounce values (5% and 57%); Facebook accounts for 17. This does not identify
   the exact publications or why the visits differ.
3. Facebook's 48.3% Côte d'Ivoire follower share and Instagram's 43.3% France
   follower share contradict using a single platform stereotype as a persona.
4. Discovery dominates (92.2% Facebook non-follower views; 98.2% new YouTube
   monthly viewers): every piece must make sense to a newcomer.
5. The 12-comment TikTok sample contains questions, disagreement and personal
   accounts; the volume of comments cannot be substituted for comprehension.
6. Source-first prose, vague references and absolute wording are editorial issues
   identified in the repository review. No causal analytics test of that wording
   has been run, so these figures do not prove it reduced performance.
7. Mobile carries 303 site visitors; its 141s duration against desktop's 571s
   supports checking mobile reading before making stronger claims.
8. The campaign mismatch and combined Meta counters prevent a clean post-to-site
   comparison. Repair interpretation and joins before ranking conversion.

## Handoffs

### For /ethniafrica-experience-optimizer

- Test whether readers on 320–430px can find the explanation, the source and an
  onward step; then check tablet and desktop. Mercator and About are candidates,
  with 23 and 9 visitors respectively, not proven navigation failures.
- Inspect source-link instrumentation and goal registration in a separate
  test-first change before claiming to measure personal research. Preserve consent.
- Reconcile sitemap-listed versus visited names/people paths before recommending merges,
  removals or new pages. Do not equate sitemap coverage with corpus completeness.

### For /ethniafrica-content-strategist

- Use the new personas by reader need. Preserve diaspora priority while making
  room for residents and carriers of local accounts; no invented demographic identity.
- Pair the next comparable drafts with a small comprehension check and a bounded
  comment review, following the [measurement protocol](editorial-measurement.md).
- Keep references precise and after the explanation; preserve oral provenance
  and competing interpretations in the spoken/card text, not just a final note.
- Reconcile the Bouët-Willaumez campaign and the two Mande editions before using
  their traffic as publication-level performance. An unresolved join stays unresolved.
- Do not translate the leading-post sample into a new cadence or format ban.

## Limits and remaining work

Facebook/Instagram comment text, TikTok per-post retention, Instagram profile link
taps, full LinkedIn per-post performance and all-publication reconciliation were
not collected. The bounded TikTok comments are not a substitute for those samples.
No interview, comprehension experiment, causal wording comparison or complete
historical-claim review was performed. Missing metrics remain unavailable. All
four user-provided services were reached successfully; X and LinkedIn were also
read for context. No social publishing or messaging took place.
