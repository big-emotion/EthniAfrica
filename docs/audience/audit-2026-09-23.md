6. The mobile/desktop duration gap is 4.5× (148 s vs 660 s).

   the project has already published.
8. TikTok and YouTube carry no rows in `sources` despite 33.5 K and 16.8 K views.
9. Patronymes: 44 visited of 797, unchanged.
10. Comment text on Facebook and Instagram has gone unread for three reports.

  la côte d'ivoire »; check that the same query typed on `/fr/atlas/recherche`
