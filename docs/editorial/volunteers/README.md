## Why these are written in French

They are addressed to francophone volunteers, like the site's own copy. This
index, aimed at maintainers, follows the repository's English rule.

## Decisions carried from the operator (2026-10-03)

- The idea stage is where volunteers help most: subject, outline, narrative
  and **images**, the slowest step. The operator keeps structure and render.
- New fiches come from a fill-in template; the operator integrates them.
- A trained volunteer may approve `referenced`-tier sources and check fiches
  alone. Classing a source `official` and ruling on a contested claim stay with
  the operator (the second half is this draft's reading; confirm it).
- Claude and git are welcome, never required. The two working modes are
  equally valid.

## How the template relates to what exists

The five Google Forms of [the contribution forms draft](../contribution-forms-draft-2026-09-22.md)
already collect the same material per class. The template does not replace
them; it is the offline equivalent a volunteer fills over several sessions, and
it adds what the 2026-10-03 re-centring of fiches on the name requires: **the
history of each form** (who gave it, since when) and **a page for every
source**. Contributors are not asked to tier their own sources, as in the forms
draft: the tier is decided at validation.

When the name-centred fiche model lands (a history per form), the template's
section 3 is the part to align with it.

## Open before sharing

1. **Hand-off channel.** Where a volunteer sends a dossier or a fiche without
   git (an email address, a shared folder). Both sheets say « écrivez à
   l'opérateur » until this is fixed.
2. **What a volunteer gets in return.** Credit on publications and a mission
   certificate are proposed in the research sheet and marked « à confirmer ».
3. **Onboarding session.** Both sheets promise one; its format (video call,
   written guide) is not decided.
4. **Intake cap.** How many dossiers or fiches can wait for review at
   once before intake pauses (raised in the brief's review).
