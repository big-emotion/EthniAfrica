# Source contract — remediation P3

Written 2026-09-30, before any P3 code, as the plan requires. It records what
changes in how a source is assessed and shown, what does not, and which part is
still a proposal. The operator's direction is in the plan's _Governing intent_;
everything below that goes beyond it is **my proposal for review**, and says so.

## Principle

Assess **support for the specific claim**, expose the source's provenance and
limits, and stop presenting an institution-based percentage as a proxy for truth.
Do not replace 1.0 / 0.7 / 0.4 with another arbitrary hierarchy, and do not
retier the corpus wholesale.

## What does not change

- `tier`, `source_kind` and the stored confidence score stay, with their values,
  so existing API consumers and the database constraints keep working. They stop
  being the public verdict; they are not deleted.
- Consent, rights status and withdrawal are separate from any assessment of a
  claim, and are never relaxed. A rejected or withdrawn account stays out.
- Search relevance may still order results by tier; ranking is not suppression.
- The quiz keeps its bounded oral-source exception (DEC-055).

## Confluence — proposed supersession notes (not written)

Each would be an appended note under the existing clause, linking DEC-055 and
this contract, with the clause itself left readable as history:

- **REQ-092** — « rejects or sends blogs, ordinary social posts, Wikipedia and
  aggregators to review » is superseded by DEC-055: they are published and
  labelled, and a traceable source is what is required.
- **DEC-011** — « prohibited factual evidence » for those categories: same.
- **DEC-050** — the name-indexing threshold by tier: superseded by D6, pending
  your approval of D6.
- **DEC-052, REQ-161/162, ARCH-022** — the approved-narrative precondition and
  the carrier deduplication are superseded by DEC-055 §6 (already stated there);
  consent is not superseded.
- **REQ-133** — « griot » wording for oral surname accounts: superseded by D1.

Nothing here is marked Approved, and no new identifier is invented.

## What this pass does not claim

No reader was asked how they read the chip. No oral source was collected. The
migration for D5 is not written. The change to the sitemap is untested against
search-engine behaviour. Deployed data was not read.
