# Editorial remediation — closure ledger

Implements [the remediation plan](../remediation-plan-2026-09-30.md) against the
33 findings of [the contradictions audit](../contradictions-2026-09-30/README.md).
The audit stays dated evidence; **this ledger is where a finding is closed**, with
evidence, or is shown to be inapplicable or superseded. A finding is never marked
resolved because a checker went green: the row says what was read and by whom.

- Frozen cases every phase is reviewed against: [review-cases.md](review-cases.md).
- The source contract P3 works to, with its proposals marked as such:
  [source-contract.md](source-contract.md).
- Status vocabulary: `open` · `in progress` · `resolved` · `superseded` ·
  `inapplicable`. Nothing else. A limitation that survives resolution is written
  in the last column, not hidden by a stronger status.
- Occurrence-level rows for content batches (C18–C24) are added under
  [Content batches](#content-batches) as each batch is reviewed. The retained
  seeds live in the audit CSVs and are not copied here.

## Precedence

Recorded 2026-09-30 on the operator's instruction. Where an older instruction
conflicts with the decisions written in the plan's _Governing intent_, the plan's
decisions prevail and the older clause is **replaced or explicitly retired**, not
worked around. Concretely:

1. `docs/editorial/reader-facing-register.md` owns editorial wording, source use,
   uncertainty and the operational research method.
2. `docs/editorial/audience-personas.md` owns the four reader needs.
3. `docs/audience/editorial-measurement.md` owns metrics and their limits.
4. `CLAUDE.md` is the agent entry point and carries no independent editorial or
   source-policy table; `AGENTS.md` stays its concise pointer.
5. Confluence owns technical requirements and decisions. A clause it still holds
   that conflicts with the above is annotated with a successor link (P0/P3), never
   silently overridden, and a Pending decision is never marked Approved here.

## Content batches

positive with a reason, or superseded with a verified replacement — and
inaccessible material is listed as **uncovered**, not as clean. A pilot is the
first batch, not a substitute for the remaining inventory.
