# Editorial strategy — names, relationships and connected series

The operator's direction, confirmed on 2026-09-22, is to understand Africa
through its names, with human relationships as the underlying subject. Start
with the [confirmed essay](../essais/comprendre-les-societes-par-les-noms-2026-09-22.md).

On 2026-09-25, the operator approved
[Mémoires sonores](../../design/gabarits-social/MEMOIRES-SONORES.md), a musical
series within EthniAfrica, limited to TikTok and Instagram. Its six-card
reference records the Sunday cadence and opening subjects. These two social
accounts may cover more than the website; the site's remit remains unchanged.

The [production ledger](../../productions/README.md) owns cadence and actual
publication records. [GABARITS-SOCIAL](../../design/gabarits-social/GABARITS-SOCIAL.md)
owns rendering and platform formats. This directory contains no publication
approval and does not create scheduled posts. Work on English editorial content
is a later capacity decision, separate from the site's existing translation and
locale-publication rules.
