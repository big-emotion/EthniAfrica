## Measurement first — three blockers

1. **Put the direct, tagged fiche URL in every Facebook caption.** The three
   Meta reels whose caption carried a direct URL drew 4 (Mandé), 5
   (Bouët-Willaumez) and 1 (Mandé correction) link clicks; the Guinée reel,
   captioned « lien en bio », drew **0 link clicks on 150,824 views**, and its
   follow-up 0 on 14,666. Facebook is the network where a link in the post is
   normal. Build it with `social/tools/link-builder/`.
2. **Record the Guinée « vingt-neuf peuples » video as published.** The ledger
   still lists it as never published while it is the period's most-viewed
   piece (150,824 Meta views). A plan cannot use as a comparable what the ledger
   does not know.
3. **The result-page click measurement** is broken since 2026-09-18 (audit,
   Site actions). It is being specified as a ticket; until it ships, the site
   cannot say whether a post's visitors found an answer.

## Social plan

**Volume: back inside the envelope.** `docs/productions/README.md` sets four to
six distinct pieces a week. Meta counted 178 pieces published on
2026-09-27 → 10-03. On TikTok, most of the ~28 posts of 1–3 October gained 1 to
10 views in the following four days, while the 8 posts of 5–6 October reached
244–934 views in one to two days. **Directive: at most two TikTok posts a day,
four to six distinct pieces a week**, held for two weeks so the next audit can
compare like with like. Cause not proven: subject, format and hour moved at the
same time.

**Third-party clips go to Instagram and Facebook, not TikTok.** Same file, same
day: Errol Musk clip 4,056 Instagram views vs 11 on TikTok; Sunjata clip 5,344
vs 31; toguna 772 vs 30; Fela 472 vs 23.

**One reader-question piece a week.** « La Guinée a-t-elle été la première ? »
answered a question asked under the Guinée video and had the week's best start
on three networks: 14,666 Meta views and 339 shares in two days, 934 TikTok
views, 163 YouTube views in 48 h. One case, so it is run as a weekly test: pick
the question from the comments of the week's leaders, answer it only if the
corpus or a consulted source answers it, and say who asked (« La question est
arrivée sous notre vidéo… »).
