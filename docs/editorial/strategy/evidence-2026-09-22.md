## Corrections to carry forward

- The audit's Facebook titles are offset from the rows' figures. The 75K row
  belongs to Mandé, the 49K row to Bouët-Willaumez. The Instagram 15K row is
  Keïta/Coulibaly. A separate 76,600-view row is titled only “Votre reel”;
  do not assign it to a subject without opening and identifying it.
- TikTok is not idle since Sep 16: Sep 21 publications are visible. Do not
  use the audit's inactivity claim to justify volume.
- The audit's approximately 0.07% conversion estimate adds incompatible views
  and periods. Retire that ratio; four tagged site visitors establish limited
  attribution, not a complete conversion rate or a proven upstream cause.
- A view proves neither understanding nor historical accuracy. Average watch
  percentage is not three-second retention. Missing retention does not support
  a claim that the new introduction has improved it.
