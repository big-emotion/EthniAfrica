countries, **place** (new), patronymes, languages — one form per category, each
opening on a single dispatch question (new entry vs. completing an existing
one). This is a draft to review and adjust before anything is built in Google
Forms or on the site; nothing here is implemented.

## 0. What already exists, and why this is not a duplicate

`/{lang}/contribute` already embeds an on-site form
(`ContributionForm.tsx` + `ContributionFormFields.tsx`) that posts to
`/api/v2/flags` — the same moderation queue a fiche's "report an error" dialog
uses, reviewed at `/fr/admin/sources`. It only covers three categories
(`people`, `country`, `language_family`), and each one asks for three or four
fields: an id, a main name, and one or two extras (a language family for a
people, an etymology and naming actor for a country). It asks nothing about
appellations, exonyms, origins, sources, or eras — the material the search
result page (REQ-178) actually shows a reader.

That gap is what this draft closes. The five templates below are not a
redesign of that form; they are a richer, naming-focused intake the on-site
form does not attempt, scoped to what the result page's grammar
(`docs/design/search-result-charter.md`, §3 ter) is built to display. A
Google Forms pilot lets the site collect this material without committing to
a UI build before knowing whether the volume and quality of answers justify
one.

**One corroborating detail**: the result page's own copy already anticipates
a fourth thing alongside a people, a language and a name — `src/lib/i18n/copy/nameAnswer.ts`'s
`unknownNameBody` reads « Si ce nom est le vôtre, ou celui d'un peuple, d'une
langue ou d'un lieu que vous connaissez, dites-le-nous. » **Lieu** is named in
the site's own voice before it exists as a corpus class.

## 1. Why Google Forms, and its limits

Recommended as a **pilot**, not a permanent architecture:

- No code, works on mobile out of the box, answers land in a Google Sheet a
  curator can work from directly.
- **No embedding.** An iframe would need `frame-src` opened in the CSP
  (`src/middleware.ts`) and would load Google's cookies before the consent
  banner, ahead of Plausible's own load-after-consent discipline. Link out
  instead.
- **No file upload without a Google account.** Excludes contributors without
  one. Ask for a link (a scan, a recording, a published source) instead of a
  file.
- **Answers do not enter the corpus by themselves.** A curator (the
  `afrik-curator` skill) turns each row into a sourced, tiered JSON proposal
  against the relevant strict model, exactly as a hand-written enrichment
  would. Forms is an intake funnel, not a publishing path — the Source Tier
  Policy and the reader-facing register apply to whatever eventually reaches
  a fiche, not to the raw answers in the Sheet.
- **Consent.** An explicit checkbox for the CC-BY-SA-style licence the
  footer already promises the reader, and an optional email only for a
  follow-up question.

## 2. The shared backbone

Every template opens the same way and closes the same way; only the middle
differs by category. This mirrors the result page's own three movements
(§3 bis of the charter: the answer, what the atlas holds, what it owes) so a
contributor answering these questions is, in effect, filling the same blanks
the page later shows a reader.

**Q0 — Dispatch (all forms, first question, required)**

> Cette fiche existe-t-elle déjà dans l'atlas ?
> ○ Non, je propose une nouvelle fiche
> ○ Oui, je complète ou corrige une fiche existante

A `Oui` branches to a short identification block (the entity's name or ID as
currently published, and a link to its page if known) before continuing into
the same question set either way — the fields to fill are the same whether
they land on a blank fiche or amend one.

**Closing block (all forms, last section)**

> - Vos sources (un lien par source ; au moins une pour toute affirmation
>   nouvelle)
> - Ce que vous ne savez pas (silence assumé, pas une case à cocher pour
>   faire vite)
> - Votre e-mail (facultatif — pour une question de suivi seulement)
> - ☐ J'accepte que ma contribution soit publiée sous la licence de l'atlas
>   et attribuée à ce que j'ai indiqué ci-dessus (ou de façon anonyme)

The "ce que vous ne savez pas" field exists on purpose: the corpus treats a
declared silence as different from an unasked question (`competing-appellations`,
CLAUDE.md's Colonial terminology section). A form that only rewards filled
fields trains contributors to guess rather than to say "je ne sais pas".

**Source question, asked once per claim, not once per form.** Each
naming-related question below is paired with "Source de cette information ?"
rather than collecting one undifferentiated source list at the end, because
the Source Tier Policy ties confidence to which claim a source backs, not to
the fiche as a whole. A curator resolves the tier later (official /
référencée / non vérifiée); the contributor is never asked to self-tier —
that judgment is editorial, not theirs to make.

## 3. Peoples (`peuple`, `PPL_*`)

Maps to `public/modele-peuple.json` → `content.appellations`, which is what
`readNaming()`'s `fromAppellations()` reads for the result page.

| Question (fr)                                                                               | Maps to                              | Required                           |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- |
| Nom principal du peuple                                                                     | `nameMain` / `appellations.mainName` | Yes                                |
| Comment ce peuple se nomme-t-il lui-même (dans sa langue) ?                                 | `appellations.selfAppellation`       | Yes¹                               |
| Quels autres noms lui donne-t-on (colons, voisins, administrations) ? Un par ligne          | `appellations.exonyms[]`             | No — explicit "aucun connu" option |
| D'où viennent ces autres noms — qui les a donnés, pourquoi ?                                | `appellations.originOfExonyms`       | If exonyms given                   |
| L'un de ces noms pose-t-il un problème (péjoratif, colonial, contesté) ? Lequel, pourquoi ? | `appellations.whyProblematic`        | No                                 |
| Qui utilise quel nom aujourd'hui ?                                                          | `appellations.contemporaryUsage`     | No                                 |
| Pays où l'on trouve ce peuple aujourd'hui                                                   | `currentCountries[]`                 | Yes                                |
| Famille linguistique (si connue)                                                            | `languageFamilyId`                   | No                                 |
| Langue principale                                                                           | `content.languages.mainLanguage`     | No                                 |

¹ Required _when known_; an explicit "auto-appellation non documentée" option
stands in for silence rather than leaving the field blank, matching the
autonym requirement the editorial gate enforces from `confidence >= medium`.

## 4. Countries (`pays`, ISO 3166-1 alpha-3)

Maps to `public/modele-pays.json` — naming lives at the fiche root
(`etymology`, `nameOriginActor`) plus `content.historicalNames`, which is
what `fromCountry()` reads. Countries are the one class where the corpus
already dates its naming (100 % of 54, per the data-shape measurement), so
this template is the only one that asks for dates by design, not as an
afterthought.

| Question (fr)                                                                                                                                                                  | Maps to                                 | Required    |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- | ----------- |
| Pays (nom usuel en français)                                                                                                                                                   | `nameFr`                                | Yes         |
| D'où vient ce nom — origine et sens du mot ?                                                                                                                                   | `etymology`                             | Yes¹        |
| Qui ou quel peuple/administration est à l'origine de ce nom ?                                                                                                                  | `nameOriginActor`                       | No          |
| Anciens noms du pays, avec leurs dates si vous les connaissez (ex. « Haute-Volta, 1919–1960 »)                                                                                 | `content.historicalNames.formerNames[]` | No          |
| Comment ce nom (ou ses formes antérieures) a-t-il changé — Antiquité, Moyen Âge, période précoloniale, colonisation, aujourd'hui ? Une réponse par période que vous connaissez | `content.historicalNames.{era}`         | No, per era |

¹ Following the assertion-certainty rule (CLAUDE.md, "Assertion tracks
certainty"): if you know of more than one account of the name's origin, list
each one on its own line rather than picking one — a curator preserves all of
them, attributed, none crowned.

## 5. Place (`lieu`) — a new class, not yet a corpus type

**This is the one template with no existing strict model, loader, or
`SearchEntityType` entry to map to.** `public/modele-*.json` has no
`modele-lieu.json`; `SearchEntityType` in `src/types/afrik-frontend.ts` lists
`country | people | language | languageFamily | person | patronyme` and
nothing else. Building this form collects real material, but publishing it
requires corpus work first: a new id namespace (e.g. `LOC_XXXXX`, alongside
`PPL_`/`FLG_`/`PAT_`), a strict model, a loader
(`src/lib/afrik/loaders/*JsonLoader.ts`), a Supabase table, and a
`readNaming()` branch before anything filled through this form can reach the
result page. Until then, submissions accumulate in the Sheet as raw material
for that future class — this section should go back to `/afrik-curator` or
`/ethniafrica-spec` as a proposal before the form goes live, not straight to
Google Forms.

The questions below follow the same naming grammar as the other four (a name,
its self-given form, its imposed forms, who named it, what that raises) on
the working assumption that a place — a region, a site, a former polity's
seat, a colonial-era toponym — answers "where does this name come from" the
same way a people or a country does.

| Question (fr) draft                                                                          | Notes                                                 |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------- |
| Nom du lieu, tel qu'on le trouve aujourd'hui                                                 | working "nameMain"                                    |
| Type de lieu (ville, région, site historique, ancien royaume, autre)                         | needed to scope the class before it is built          |
| Nom local / nom que les habitants utilisent, si différent                                    | working "selfAppellation"                             |
| Quels autres noms ce lieu a-t-il portés (colonial, précédent, cartographique) ? Un par ligne | working "exonyms"                                     |
| D'où vient chacun de ces noms — qui l'a donné, quand ?                                       | working "origin", per form, not one shared field      |
| Pays actuel                                                                                  | ISO 3166-1 alpha-3, links to an existing `pays` fiche |
| Peuple(s) associé(s) à ce lieu, si pertinent                                                 | links to an existing `PPL_*`                          |

## 6. Patronymes (`nom-patronyme`, `PAT_*`)

Maps to `public/modele-nom-patronyme.json`. Patronymes already have the
one-record-per-form shape the other classes are converging toward
(CLAUDE.md, "the corpus's own patronyme model already has the shape the
other four are converging toward") — `spellings[]` each carrying their own
`attestations[]`, which is what `fromSpellings()` reads. The form should
collect one spelling per line, not a single free-text name field.

| Question (fr)                                                                                                                                         | Maps to                                                               | Required |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Nom (patronyme, nom de clan, nisba…)                                                                                                                  | `nameMain`                                                            | Yes      |
| Orthographes attestées de ce nom, une par ligne, avec le pays où vous l'avez vue écrite ainsi                                                         | `spellings[].spelling` + `.attestations[].countryId`                  | Yes¹     |
| Type de système de nomination (nom de clan, patronymique non héréditaire, nisba, nom de louange, clan totémique) — si vous ne savez pas, laissez vide | `nameSystem`                                                          | No       |
| Comment se transmet-il (patrilinéaire, matrilinéaire, les deux, électif, non héréditaire, autre)                                                      | `transmissionMode`                                                    | No       |
| Origine du nom — tradition orale, chronique écrite, reconstruction linguistique. Qui la raconte, que dit-elle ?                                       | `origin.oralTraditions[].claim` (+ `griot`) or `.writtenChronicles[]` | No       |
| Peuple(s) portant ce nom                                                                                                                              | `peoples[].peopleId`                                                  | No       |
| Pays où ce nom est porté                                                                                                                              | `countries[].countryId`                                               | No       |
| Alliances à plaisanterie connues avec un autre patronyme (et leur nom, ex. « sanankuya »)                                                             | `alliances[]`                                                         | No       |

¹ At least one spelling is required; every subsequent field is optional
because the patronyme model itself makes almost everything else optional
(`transmissionMode`, `origin`, `alliances` are all nullable in the strict
model).

## 7. Languages (`langue`, ISO 639-3)

Maps to `public/modele-langue.json`, read from the fiche root (or from
`content` once loaded through the API — see `naming.ts`'s comment on why
`readNaming()` reads both).

| Question (fr)                                                                     | Maps to               | Required                      |
| --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Nom de la langue en français                                                      | `nameFr`              | Yes                           |
| Code ISO 639-3, si vous le connaissez                                             | `isoCode639_3` / `id` | No — a curator can look it up |
| Autres noms attestés de cette langue, un par ligne                                | `alternateNames[]`    | No                            |
| L'un de ces noms pose-t-il un problème (colonial, péjoratif) ? Lequel, pourquoi ? | `whyProblematic`      | No                            |
| Orthographes alternatives attestées                                               | `spellingAliases[]`   | No                            |
| Famille linguistique (si connue)                                                  | `familyId`            | No                            |
| Peuple(s) parlant cette langue                                                    | `peoples[].peopleId`  | No                            |

## 8. Placement on the Contribute page

Following mobile-first review order (430 px first):

- A new section, **after** the existing on-site contribution form and
  **before** "Documentation API" — it is a heavier, slower path than the
  quick-flag form above it, so it should not be the first thing a mobile
  reader meets.
- One short paragraph explaining the difference from the form above it
  ("le formulaire ci-dessus corrige une fiche rapidement ; ceux-ci servent à
  documenter un nom en profondeur, peuple par peuple, pays par pays") —
  otherwise a reader meets two contribution mechanisms with no stated reason
  to pick one.
- Five stacked cards or a simple list (mobile: one column, full width; ≥720 px:
  two columns), each naming the category in plain French (**Peuples**,
  **Pays**, **Lieux**, **Patronymes**, **Langues** — never the internal
  identifiers `PPL_`/`FLG_`/`PAT_`, per the reader-facing register), a one-line
  description, and a button linking out to that category's Google Form in a
  new tab (`target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`, matching the existing
  GitHub link pattern on the same page).
- The **Lieu** card should carry a visible "nouveau" or "en construction"
  marker, since — unlike the other four — nothing published today reads what
  it collects (§5).

## 9. Open points before building anything

1. **Confirm the six questions per template above** — trim, reorder, or add
   before they become live Google Forms; this table is the thing to edit,
   not the eventual Form.
2. **`lieu`'s scope.** "Ville", "région historique", "ancien royaume",
   "site" are one candidate typology; it may be narrower (e.g. limited to
   former polities and colonial-era place names, which already have partial
   coverage through `content.kingdoms[]` on a country fiche) or broader. This
   is a modeling decision, best made with `/afrik-curator` or
   `/ethniafrica-spec` before the form asks anything.
3. **Curator cadence.** Who reads the Sheets, how often, and whether a
   contributor is ever told their submission became a fiche — the invitation
   on the result page promises "dites-le-nous", not silence afterward.
