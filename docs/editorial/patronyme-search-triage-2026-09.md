# Surname searches with no answer — triage of September 2026

Readers typed these strings into the search box in September 2026 (log window
2026-09-01 to 2026-09-29) and the corpus had no surname fiche for them. Each was
triaged: real surname, people, place, typo or noise. A fiche is created only
where at least one substantive, sourced element exists beyond a count of
bearers; an incidence figure from a genealogy aggregator is not a source for an
origin, a meaning or a people.

This is the working record of what was tried. It is the list to reopen when a
new source appears (an oral account, a community contribution, a published
study), and the list a reader-facing "not yet documented" answer can rest on.
