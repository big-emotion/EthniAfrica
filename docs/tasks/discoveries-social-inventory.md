# Découvertes social-library inventory

This is an implementation audit, not a publication manifest or a new specification. The private library stays outside Git. The project's `social/tools/paths.mjs` resolver loaded its configured workshop and finished-post roots from `.env.local`; no workstation path was guessed or copied into this document. Counts below are a read-only snapshot on 2026-09-13.

The `Publie` shelf contains 28 post folders: 12 have ordered three-format carousel image sets, and 16 have video files without a comparable carousel set. The nine 2026-09-11 carousel folders have 5–8 cards each, three rendered sizes per card, and a local `SOURCES.md`; their `post.md` captions still contain an image-credit placeholder that needs individual review. The three 2026-09-12 carousel folders are `akan-quatre-noms` (6 cards), `amazigh-neuf-pays` (8 cards), and `dioula-cinq-pays` (6 cards). The Akan post itself says **NON PUBLIABLE** and **TITRE NON APPROUVÉ** despite its shelf placement. It is excluded from a candidate feed.

The workshop contains eight `cards.json` manifests. Their schema uses `cartes` and French copy; none declares English/locale fields. Two campaigns match a finished carousel folder exactly (`akan-quatre-noms`, `amazigh-neuf-pays`). `dioula-cinq-pays` and workshop campaign `dioula-sans-territoire` may be related, but the identifiers differ; the mapping is unverified. The other five manifests are not direct matches to finished carousel image sets. A folder name or common subject is not a source identity.

The first Amazigh carousel master was visually inspected: it is a real 1080×1350 social composition with a relevant mountain photograph, lower-left type, visible credit and the EthniAfrica mark. Its workshop manifest describes eight cards, individual image credit, crop and CC BY-SA 4.0 output licence; `SOURCES.md` supplies original file URLs and licence readings. This proves the asset is present and traceable, not that its French-only text may be shipped in a bilingual reader. The 2026-09-11 sets likewise require a card-by-card source and approval check, not blanket import from the published shelf.

Before any carousel enters the destination, reconcile the finished master to its workshop record and final approval; check every card's source and image licence, ordered assets and caption; provide reviewed French and English content and a localized transcript; verify atlas destinations; and prepare a repository-managed public asset derivative with recorded rights. If an approved campaign remains unavailable, exclude that publication while building the independent reader. Do not label the three-slide handoff demonstration as a YouTube import or production post.
