## Approval evidence

Do not prefill approvals. Each real approval must identify its scope, exact artifact
version/hash, the approving person and the actual statement plus a retrievable file
or session/message reference and date when known. Preserve quotations verbatim.
If the evidence cannot be recovered, mark it pending rather than asserting approval.

On resume, compare the current bytes with the approved versions. Do not treat a
technical path-only adjustment as a narrative rewrite; document allowed adjustments
in the brief. A new asset, claim or geometry needs its affected review. Record asset
rights and voice rights in the source/release records, never infer them from this table.
