# Captions for the networks (`descriptions.md`)

The shape the operator published for the Sankara, Mitchell and Mangope reels.
Public text: it follows `docs/editorial/reader-facing-register.md` and must pass
`npm run check:publication -- descriptions.md`.

## Rules

- **Open on the subject, as a real question** the excerpt answers or raises
  (often the speaker's own question). No invented fear, no promise the excerpt
  does not keep.
- **Attribute every thesis** to the speaker: « avance que », « pour elle »,
  « selon lui ». Nothing the speaker argues is stated in EthniAfrica's voice.
- **Present the speaker with a verified role** (the same one as the identity
  box). No date of the interview unless it is known.
- **Short quotations** only, from our own captions, in « ».
- **Close on a question to the reader**, then the credit line: « Extrait de …,
  sous-titré en français par EthniAfrica. » (or « traduit avec son
  autorisation » when the author gave it).
- **Links**: not clickable in a TikTok or Instagram caption. There, tell the
  reader what to search on YouTube. Put the URL in clear on YouTube, Facebook,
  X and LinkedIn.
- **Accounts**: @handles on Instagram when the operator gave them; on TikTok
  only if their TikTok accounts were checked.
- Three to five hashtags, lower case, no accents needed except where usual.
